Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01/05/2025

At Mynah, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app. By using Mynah, you agree to the terms outlined below. If you do not agree, please refrain from using the app.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account or use the app, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Email Address and Password: Collected via Firebase Authentication to securely create and manage your account.
  • Name and Phone Number: Stored securely in Firestore, the data is collected during registration and used to personalize your experience..
  • Home Location and Address: Stored securely in Firestore, used to associate locust sightings with farm areas..
  • Farm Location and Address: Stored securely in Firestore, used to understand the user base's distribution.

1.2 Images and Files

  • Camera Access: The app allows users to take pictures of locust. These images are sent to a server for processing.
  • Gallery Access: Users can upload locust images from their device’s gallery, which are then sent to the server for processing.
  • GPS Location: Collected at the time of image capture to geotag locust sightings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:
  • Account Authentication and Profile Management:Your personal details are stored using Firebase Authentication and Firestore to securely manage your account.
  • History Tracking: Classification results, images, and associated location data are stored in Firebase Firestore and/or Hive for offline access and historical tracking.
  • Image Analysis & AI Processing: Captured/uploaded images are sent to a server hosted on Google Cloud Run. AI models process these images to detect and classify locust types (e.g., gregarious or solitary).

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except in the following cases:
  • Firebase: Used for authentication and securely storing your personal information.
  • Firestore: Used to store classification history, location data, and metadata.
  • Firebase Storage: Stores locust images and makes them accessible via download URLs.
  • Google Cloud Run: Used to host the server where images are processed using AI models. Only locust images are sent to the server for analysis; no additional personal data is shared.

4. Permissions Requested by the App

To provide the core functionality of the app, we request the following permissions:
  • Camera Access: Allows users to take pictures of Locust for analysis.
  • Gallery Access: Allows users to upload images from their device for AI processing.
  • Location Access (GPS): Required to geotag locust sightings for tracking and research purposes.
  • Internet Access: Required for communication with Firebase, Firestore, Firebase Storage, and the Google Cloud Run server for processing.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal data, including profile information and locust analysis history, for as long as your account is active. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at xraid2025@gmail.com.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data using Firebase's security infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. User Control Over Data

You can control your personal data in the following ways:
  • Account Deletion: You can request to delete your account and all associated data by contacting us.
  • Profile Information: You can update or delete your profile information, such as your name and birthdate, directly within the app.

8. Age Restrictions

Our app is designed for users aged 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a user under the age of 13 has created an account, we will promptly delete their data. Please contact us if you believe we have collected personal information from a user under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
